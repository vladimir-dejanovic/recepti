# Pork Belly Burnt Ends - BBQ

Pork Belly Burnt Ends are tender cubes of pork belly that are slowly cooked until they are buttery and delicious sticky. The perfect sweet-savvy BBQ snack you can’t stay away from

- 120 min prepare

## Ingredients for 4 people

- 1 kg pork belly without rind
- 50 g brown sugar
- 50 g butter
- 14 tbsp Jumbo's BBQ sauce korean style
- 1 casserole (18 x 28 cm)
- 1 apple-smoke wood

## Preparation

Put these delicious Pork Belly Burnt Ends on the table in 7 steps.

- Cut the pork belly into cubes of about 4x4 cm and season with the rub.
- Prepare the BBQ for indirect grilling (a technique where you do not put the meat above the coals, but next to it) and heat to 120 °C. Place a cube of apple-smokewood between the coals.
- Cook the pork belly for about 1 hour.
- Put the pork belly cubes in a baking dish with the butter, caster sugar and half the BBQ sauce. Cover with aluminum foil, cook for another 1 hour and remove the bowl from the BBQ.
- Increase the temperature of the BBQ to 180 °C. Remove the aluminum foil from the bowl and brush the cubes well with the resulting sauce.
- Put the bowl back on the BBQ for about 20 minutes. Brush the meat every 5 minutes until the sauce is sticky.
- Season with extra rub and serve.

Tip! The burnt ends are an incredibly tasty snack to start your BBQ evening with or as a supplement to your drink board.
