# Moink balls - BBQß


Moinkballs are the ultimate drink classic of the BBQ: juicy beef meatballs, wrapped with crispy breakfast bacon and lacquered with a sticky layer of barbecue sauce. By slowly cooking them on the BBQ they get a delicious smoky taste and they stay extra tender inside.

- 60 min prepare

## Ingredients for 30 people

- 1 kg Beef mince
- 30 slices of breakfast bacon
- 2 pieces of eggs
- 40 g breadcrumbs
- 1 piece onion
- 50 g Parmesan cheese
- 200 ml BBQ sauce

## Preparation

Put these delicious Moink balls on the table in 10 steps.

- Slice the onion as finely as possible.
- Mix the mince with the onion, eggs, breadcrumbs and knead this well.
- Season the mince with BXC Beef Blend and the parmesan cheese.
- Form small meatballs of the mince.
- Wrap a slice of breakfast bacon around each meatball.
- Light the bbq and bring it to a temperature of 150 degrees. With an indirect setup in it so that you can cook the Moinkballs without direct contact with the heat source.
- Cook the moinkballs to a core temperature of 54 degrees.
- Increase the temperature of the bbq to 180 degrees.
- Place the moinkballs back on the grid and brush them all around with bbq sauce.
- Let the bbq sauce thicken until a nice shiny and sticky layer is created around the balls. 

Tip! Serve the Moinkballs at the start of your bbq meal or serve as a snack on a drink board.
