# Shotgun Shells - BBQ

Stuffed cannelloni wrapped with bacon and slowly cooked on the BBQ for an irresistibly smoky taste. Painted with sticky barbecue sauce: a real darling of the grill.

- 60 min prepare

## Ingredients for 4 people

- 500 g Beef mince
- 1 piece of shallot
- 2 tbsp capers
- 2 pieces Pickles
- 50 g Grasped cheddar
- 24 slices of breakfast bacon
- 250 g Cannelloni
- 14 tbsp jumbo's BBQ sauce texas style
- 1 piping bag
- 1 smoke wood

## Preparation

Put these delicious Shotgun Shells on the table in 7 steps.

- Slice the shallot and cut the pickles into fine cubes.
- Mix the minced meat with the shallot, pickle, capers and cheddar in a bowl. Season with the rub and knead well.
- Put the mince mixture in a piping bag and fill the cannellonis.
- Wrap a slice of breakfast bacon around each cannelloni. Set aside.
- Prepare the BBQ for indirect grilling (a technique where you do not put the meat above the coals, but next to it) and heat to 120 °C. Place a block of smoked wood between the coals.
- Place the cannellonis on the grid for about 40 minutes.
- Increase the temperature of the BBQ to 180 °C. Brush the cannellonis all around with BBQ sauce and put them back for another 10 minutes until the sauce is sticky. 

Tip! For extra spice, add some chilli flakes to the mince mixture.
