# Mochi

## sastojci
- glutionus rice flour 45g
- corn starch 10g
- sugar 30g
- milk 90g
- butter 10g

## Preping

1. in a blowdl add rice flour, corn starch sugar and milk
2. mix unilt combined, filter and transfer into a pn
3. add butter, mix and cook on low geat until soft dought is formed
4. use hand gloves, knead the mochi until strechy
5. divide into bite size pieces and coat with corn starch to prevent sticking
6. pair with chocolate sauce and crusdhed biscoff cookies
